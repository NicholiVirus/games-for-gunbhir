      RARITY & ABILITIES

      • COMMON 1 ability (Special) and a +8% damage perk · costs 100
      • RARE 2 abilities (Special + Super) and a +4% damage perk · 200
      • EPIC 3 abilities (Special + Down-Special + Super) · 400
      • LEGENDARY 3 abilities plus a unique passive (Survivor, Iron Will, Momentum, Filibuster) · 600+

      COINS

      • Every match pays coins: showing up, rounds won, wins, perfect rounds, super finishes and a CPU difficulty bonus. Clearing the Election Campaign pays a big bonus.
      • Spend coins in the Shop, or buy a locked fighter straight from the select screen (press pick twice to confirm).
      • You start with David Ben-Gurion (Rare). There are 22 fighters to recruit in total.
      • Election Night Gold skin: win 3 matches with a fighter, or buy it for 150.

      THE RULES OF ORDER

      • Block by holding away from your opponent. Crouch-block (down + away) stops sweeps; jump attacks must be blocked standing.
      • Crouch + Kick is a sweep that knocks down. Punch chains into more punches, a kick, or your Special.
      • Ability 2 types: Uppercut (rising anti-air), Counter (parry stance — strikes back if hit), or a second projectile/charge.
      • Gamepads: D-pad/stick to move, A punch, B kick, X special (hold down for Ability 2), Y super.
      • ESC pauses. M toggles music.
