      THE RULES OF ORDER

      • Block by holding away from your opponent. Crouch-block (down + away) stops sweeps; jump attacks must be blocked standing.
      • Crouch + Kick is a sweep that knocks down. Punch chains into more punches, a kick, or your Special.
      • Every hit fills your MANDATE meter. At 100% press Super for a cinematic finisher.
      • Win rounds to earn Mandates — they unlock every Prime Minister in order. Contenders and the secret boss have their own challenges.
      • Win 3 matches with a fighter to unlock their Election Night Gold skin. Toggle skins on the select screen with R / O.
      • Gamepads: D-pad/stick to move, A punch, B kick, X special, Y super. Pad 1 = P1, Pad 2 = P2.
      • ESC pauses. M toggles music.
